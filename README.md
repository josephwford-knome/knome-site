## About KnoMe

KnoMe is a patent-pending sovereign data platform — the consent layer beneath health records, academic credentials, and employer benefits. Families own their data in SOLID pods. KnoMe holds zero sensitive data; only encrypted access pointers.

**Three market verticals:**
- **KnoMe Health** — Decentralized personal health record
- **KnoMe EdTech** — Student sovereignty & the Child Success Passport
- **KnoMe@Work** — Employer health sovereignty & MIURA incentive architecture

**EU Pilot:** FAME (Future Albanian Minds Empowered) — Kosovo
