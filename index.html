About

Why we built this.

KnoMe was founded by Joseph Ford, who spent years working in healthcare and insurance before co-founding and running a learning center for a decade. Both experiences pointed to the same problem: the people whose lives are most affected by a record rarely have any real control over it.

KnoMe's first pilot is underway in Kosovo, in partnership with FAME (Future Albanian Minds Empowered), co-founded by Behar Shala, a school director in Dragash. It's the first test of whether this architecture works in practice, outside the US — and a template for taking the same approach to other countries.
