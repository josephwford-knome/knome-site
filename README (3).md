## About KnoMe

KnoMe is a patent-pending, family-owned data architecture for health, education, and work.

**Three markets, one architecture:**
- **KnoMe Health** — a personal health record that's actually yours
- **KnoMe EdTech** — a portable record of a child's whole journey
- **KnoMe@Work** — employer health benefits, reimagined

**Pilot:** FAME (Future Albanian Minds Empowered) — Kosovo
